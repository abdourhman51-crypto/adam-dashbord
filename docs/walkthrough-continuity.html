Why the catalogue had to go

The record — memory made into an object she owns

This is new in v3, and it solves a problem that dogged both earlier versions. Memory is the differentiator, but nobody buys memory — your own offer prompt says so: «لا أحد يشتري ذاكرة».

The record doesn't sell memory. It hands it over. One document, free, growing forever, that she can ask for at any moment.

Why it must be free

The instant a parent's knowledge of her own child sits behind a paywall, ADAM stops being a companion and becomes a landlord. The record is hers. It is also the single most tangible proof that this is one relationship and not a series of transactions — it does not reset, it does not expire, and it never belonged to us.

What it quietly does for retention

Every stage makes it richer. After a year it holds things no other product could reconstruct — and it was built one tap at a time, by her. Leaving means leaving that behind. That is an honest switching cost, built from delivered value rather than from lock-in.

Day 15–16the night after the stage ends

Nothing stops

This is the most important fix in v3. In v2, a Journey ended, the parent dropped back to free, and an offer arrived later — three distinct events that taught her she had been a customer for fourteen days and was now between purchases.

In v3 the stage report is delivered, and then the next evening at 21:00 the rhythm simply continues, exactly as it always has. No downgrade message. No “your Journey has ended.” Nothing to notice.

Day 1921:00 · guidance, free

ADAM decides what matters next — and charges nothing to say so

Blueprint v1 banned proactive selling on hard evidence: 8 pushed offers, 0 clicks. But a companion who never guides isn't a companion. v3 resolves this with one rule:

Guidance is free, proactive and unconditional. The transaction is pull-only.

Read what is missing from this message

  • No price. Not mentioned, not hinted.
  • No button. Nothing to tap.
  • No duration. Not framed as a package.
  • No date, no urgency, no expiry.

It is a doctor saying “I think we should look at this next.” That is care. Attaching an invoice to it, unprompted, is not — and your data already showed what happens when you do.

Why ADAM choosing is a feature, not a restriction

She is exhausted. Asking her to diagnose her own family and pick a programme is work — and it is work she came here to be relieved of. ADAM choosing, and showing his reasoning from her own logs, is the entire value of having a companion who has been watching. She can always redirect; she rarely will, because he is right and he shows why.

The long arc — the same ADAM, two years on

This is what “lifelong companion” actually buys you, and it is a stronger retention story than any subscription: not “keep paying to retain access” but “I have known your child since he was five.”

What ADAM does here that nothing else can

He reaches back two years to a solved problem and reuses its mechanism — because the underlying trait is the same child's trait. Yusuf has always needed warning before transitions. At five it was bedtime; at seven it is the school gate.

No new parent could know that. No new AI could know that. Only the one who was there.

Stages follow the child, not the customer

A 5-year-old's bedtime becomes a 7-year-old's school mornings becomes a 9-year-old's screen conflict becomes a 12-year-old's withdrawal. The relationship has no natural end date, because childhood keeps producing new stages — and each one is easier than the last, because the record keeps growing.

What changed

Six decisions, one constraint

“Journeys are chapters, not products” sounds like naming. It is six structural decisions, and leaving any one of them unchanged would have leaked the shop back in.

DecisionIf productsIf chapters
MenuPriced catalogue«أين نحن؟» — history, not inventory
Who choosesShe browses and selectsADAM proposes with reasoning; she confirms
Between stagesEnds → gap → new offerNothing pauses; no status message exists
The recordPer-Journey, resetsOne document, free, grows forever, hers
Ask language“Buy the Sibling Journey”«نكمل» — we continue
HorizonEnds with the JourneyFollows the child from 3 to 13

What she is buying

Not a programme. A period of closer attention from someone already walking with her — who arrives before the hard moment instead of after it, for as long as that particular thing is hard.

What she never does

Compare her son's sleep against her daughter's jealousy by price. The catalogue that would force that comparison does not exist.

How ADAM sells without selling

Guidance is free, proactive and unconditional — he says what matters next, with no price, no button, no date. The transaction only ever appears after she pulls it. This is the rule that reconciles “never push” with “always guide.”

Why she stays

Not because access would be revoked — nothing is revoked. Because the record keeps growing, each stage is easier than the last, and after two years ADAM knows things about her child that nobody else could reconstruct.

What is unchanged from v2

The child problem is still the hook. The objective is still declared before payment. The three-phase spine — observe, build, hold — is intact. Crisis is still never billed. The parent's transformation is still the payoff, not the pitch.

The one open blocker

Unchanged and still yours: where a parent-violence disclosure routes. Nothing in this revision touches it, and I would still not scale past a pilot without it settled.
