What changed, and what I pushed back on

The architecture

The hook

A named child problem with a duration: رحلة النوم — ١٤ يوماً. Not a promise about her identity. Your content data settles this — child-problem posts reach 525,682; parent-empathy posts reach 3,776. A 139× difference on the same account.

The payoff

Her transformation, revealed in the day-14 report as one unannounced line, and fully in the free Parent Mirror after two Journeys. Planted in act 3, harvested in act 11.

What is free

Rescue in any crisis, any topic, any country, forever. Nightly logging. The First Mirror. The Parent Mirror. Crisis is never billed — even mid-Journey, as act 6 shows.

What is paid

The programme: daily anticipation before the flashpoint, coaching that adapts to this child, pattern detection, the mid-Journey Mirror, and the final report. $10 equivalent, flat, per Journey — 14 days or 45, the price is the outcome, not the time.

Why she buys again

Because ADAM diagnoses the next problem from her own logged data, at a moment of success, and then waits. No urgency, no expiry, no funnel. Sequential Journeys are the retention engine — each tied to a felt need rather than a calendar.

The three-phase spine

Observe (demand nothing, get an honest baseline) → Build (the intervention) → Hold (ADAM fades on purpose). Phase 3 is the one nobody builds and the one that proves the change was real rather than compliance.

Challenged

Three items from the proposed list that do not survive

A Journey needs four things to work: a recurring predictable moment, a near-binary daily signal, an action doable in under ten minutes, and a behavioural rather than clinical target. Applied honestly, three candidates fail.

1 — Confidence has no 19:15

There is no flashpoint for shyness, and a parent cannot log “was he confident today?” as a binary. Every paid mechanic — the prep message, the Mirror, the objective — depends on both. Confidence is a by-product of the other Journeys and should be reported as one, not sold as a programme that cannot deliver its own mechanics.

2 — Speech is the #3 theme and must never be a Journey

98 messages. Selling a parent a “Speech Journey” risks delaying clinical referral during the window when early intervention matters most. This is the one place where the commercially attractive move is the harmful one. It gets a referral track: recognise, inform, point toward assessment, stay present — no programme, no fee.

3 — “Aggression” is two things, and one is a crisis

The 132 hitting messages split into child→sibling, child→parent, and parent→child — «المشكله ان انا مش بصرف بس انا بضرب». The first two are a Journey. The third is the crisis path. Offering a programme to a parent who has just disclosed hitting their child converts a disclosure into a sales qualification. The Journey must be gated.

4 — Launch two, not seven

289 users, one payment. Seven Journeys is seven step libraries and seven report templates built before one is proven. Evidence ranks Defiance first (83 messages, 525,682 reach, 5,998 comments — your largest content proof in existence) and Sleep second (cleanest measurable objective, best proof-of-architecture). You led with Sleep; I would build both, and nothing else until one converts.

5 — Durations are reasoning, not evidence

14 / 21 / 30 / 45 days are derived from behavioural logic, not from your data — the live system holds 21 daily_logs rows in total. Each duration is a hypothesis to be corrected by measuring where the outcome curve actually flattens. Stated plainly so nobody mistakes my reasoning for proof.
